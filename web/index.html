    SYS // ABOUT PRIORART COPILOT & AI ARCHITECTURE

    Autonomous Multi-Agent Patentability & Prior-Art Collision Intelligence
    01 / PURPOSE

    What This Website & Intelligence Platform Does

    PriorArt Copilot is a production-grade, multi-agent AI research workstation that solves the most costly bottleneck in patent prosecution: preliminary patentability screening and prior-art collision detection.

    Filing invalid patent applications costs law firms and enterprises millions in rejection fees and wasted prosecution cycles. PriorArt Copilot automatically deconstructs invention disclosures into discrete claim elements, queries global patent registries across 5 major jurisdictions, tests for 35 U.S.C. § 102 (Novelty) direct anticipation and 35 U.S.C. § 103 (Obviousness) combinations, and synthesizes audit-ready legal examination memos with strict verbatim citation traceability.

    02 / AGENT PIPELINE

    Autonomous Multi-Agent Neural Pipeline (How It Works)

    AGENT 01
    Claim Element Deconstructor

    Extracts and normalizes the invention disclosure into independent, non-overlapping claim elements, categorizing each into physical limitations, functional steps, and measurable tolerances.

    LangGraph · Gemini 2.5 Flash
    AGENT 02
    Multi-Jurisdiction Prior-Art Mesh

    Dispatches targeted search queries across 5 official registries (USPTO, EPO, WIPO, JPO, CNIPA) combining dense semantic vector embeddings and sparse BM25 keyword matching to prevent blindspots.

    Hybrid RAG · 5 Global Repos
    AGENT 03
    Element-by-Element Citation Reasoner

    Performs strict § 102 anticipation and § 103 obviousness mapping. Enforces strict zero-hallucination policies by demanding exact verbatim citations, column/paragraph numbers, and relevancy rationale.

    Strict Traceability · § 102 / § 103
    AGENT 04
    Patentability Synthesizer & Memo Generator

    Aggregates overlap matrices, computes overall threat scores (High, Mod, Low), drafts strategic prosecution recommendations, design-around pathways, and renders print-safe vector PDF reports.

    Audit-Ready Memo · PDF Export
    03 / 3D TELEMETRY

    Real-Time Geolocation & 3D Point-Cloud Radar

    The interactive 3D WebGL sphere (~8,400 Fibonacci vertices) projects Earth's continental topology and triangulates the client node in real-time. As inventions are analyzed, ballistic trajectory arcs bridge the user's origin to target patent assignee hubs (DJI Shenzhen, Boeing Chicago, Sony Tokyo, Airbus Toulouse, Fraunhofer Munich) with traveling photon pulses illustrating global citation collisions.
