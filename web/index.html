    SYS // ABOUT PRIORART COPILOT & AI ARCHITECTURE

    Autonomous Multi-Agent Patentability & Prior-Art Collision Intelligence
    01 / PURPOSE

    What This Website & Intelligence Platform Does

    PriorArt Copilot is an enterprise-grade, autonomous multi-agent AI workstation designed to eliminate the most expensive and time-consuming bottleneck in intellectual property: preliminary patentability screening, novelty clearance, and prior-art collision detection.

    Filing invalid patent applications costs inventors, enterprises, and patent attorneys thousands in official examination rejection fees, office actions, and wasted prosecution cycles. PriorArt Copilot automatically deconstructs invention disclosures into discrete claim elements, queries global patent registries across 5 major jurisdictions (USPTO, EPO, WIPO, JPO, CNIPA) alongside peer-reviewed academic literature, evaluates direct anticipation under 35 U.S.C. § 102 (Novelty) and combinational obviousness under 35 U.S.C. § 103 (Inventive Step), and synthesizes audit-ready legal examination memos with 100% verified verbatim citations.

    02 / AGENT PIPELINE

    Autonomous 4-Agent Neural Workflow (How It Works)

    AGENT 01 READER
    Claim Element Deconstructor

    Deconstructs raw technical prose into discrete, non-overlapping claim elements. Classifies physical structural limitations, functional operations, and measurable tolerances. Assigns candidate IPC/CPC classification codes and powers the interactive Claim Review step.

    LangGraph · disclosure_parser.py · Gemini 3 Flash
    AGENT 02 SEARCHER
    Multi-Jurisdiction Prior-Art Mesh

    Performs domain-specific technical patent lexicon expansion (synonyms) and dispatches targeted parallel queries per claim element across Google Patents, EPO Open Patent Services (OPS), and Semantic Scholar academic non-patent literature (NPL).

    Hybrid RAG · retrieval_agent.py · BM25 + Dense
    AGENT 03 JUDGE
    Novelty Clustering & Threat Reasoner

    Aligns discovered prior-art candidate texts against each individual claim element. Computes fine-grained semantic overlap, classifies statutory risk tiers (High § 102, Moderate § 103, Low/Safe), and isolates the inventor's defensible Novelty Gaps.

    novelty_clustering.py · 35 U.S.C. § 102 / § 103
    AGENT 04 WRITER
    Patentability Synthesizer & Memo Generator

    Assembles the interactive 2D Threat Matrix Heatmap, enforces 100% citation authenticity (demanding exact verbatim quotes and publication numbers), formulates strategic claim drafting recommendations, and renders vector PDF / Markdown clearance reports.

    report_writer.py · Zero-Hallucination · PDF Export

    04 / STATUTORY STANDARDS

    Patent Law Evaluation Framework & 2D Threat Matrix

    Every discovered reference is rigorously examined against standard patent examination doctrines:

    35 U.S.C. § 102 · ANTICIPATION (HIGH THREAT ≥90%) A single existing reference discloses all technical limitations of the claimed element. Fatal novelty bar requiring design-around.
    35 U.S.C. § 103 · OBVIOUSNESS (MODERATE THREAT 80-89%) Analogous art in related domains that an examiner could combine. Requires demonstrating unexpected results or secondary considerations.
    NOVELTY GAP · PATENTABLE TERRITORY (LOW RISK <80%) Claim elements completely unencumbered by discovered prior art. Forms the primary defensible basis for valid, enforceable patent claims.
    ZERO-HALLUCINATION VERIFICATION 100% citation authenticity verification: citations must match verified verbatim text and link to official patent office records.
